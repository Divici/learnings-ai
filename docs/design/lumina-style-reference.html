Explain the "Vanishing Gradient Problem" in deep feedforward networks.

Focus on the mathematical cause during backpropagation.

Click or Space to flip
Answer

It occurs when gradients become vanishingly small as they are multiplied together during backpropagation through many layers.

Chain Rule: ∂L/∂W = (∂L/∂y) * (∂y/∂h) * ... * (∂h/∂W)
If derivatives (like Sigmoid < 0.25) are < 1, their product approaches zero exponentially.

Solutions: Using non-saturating activation functions (ReLU), careful weight initialization (He/Glorot), or architectural changes (Residual connections / ResNets).
